Measuring Student Growth with MAP®

MAP Growth is a computer-adaptive assessment from NWEA that helps measure achievement and growth over time. ISK uses it to guide learning across reading, language usage, and mathematics.

Questions adapt to each student's responses, giving teachers a clearer view of current achievement and next learning steps.

A dashboard for growth, not just a score.

MAP Growth helps teachers see where a student is ready to stretch, where support is needed, and how progress changes across the year.

How It Works

Adaptive assessment for every learner.

Questions dynamically adjust to each student’s ability level, providing a personalised testing experience that accurately measures abilities regardless of grade level.

Adaptive Assessment

Questions dynamically adjust to each student's ability level, providing a personalised testing experience that accurately measures abilities regardless of grade level.

Global Benchmarking

Compare student performance against international standards and peer groups worldwide, based on the American education system.

Growth Tracking

Measure academic progress over time with detailed growth reports and visualisations, administered three times per academic year.

Data-Driven Instruction

Teachers analyse reports for comprehensive understanding of each student's strengths, weaknesses, and overall academic progress.

Personalised Goals

Based on assessment results, educators and students set specific learning goals, identifying areas of excellence and areas requiring improvement.

Parental Involvement

Parents can view their child's assessment results, track progress, and gain insights into academic performance through the MAP platform.

Assessment Schedule

Three assessments per academic year.

MAP Growth tests are administered at three key points during the school year, enabling ISK to track growth and adjust instruction accordingly.

Beginning of Year

Baseline assessment that establishes starting points for each student in reading, language usage, and mathematics.

Mid-Year

Mid-point check to measure growth, identify areas needing additional support, and adjust teaching strategies.

End of Year

Final assessment captures total annual growth and provides data for planning the following academic year.

Subjects Assessed

Comprehensive diagnostic coverage.

Reading

Measures comprehension, literary analysis, and informational text skills. Results pinpoint specific reading skills that need reinforcement.

Language Usage

Assesses grammar, mechanics, and writing conventions. Helps teachers target areas of language development for each student.

Mathematics

Evaluates number sense, operations, algebra, geometry, and data analysis. Identifies precise skill gaps and mastery levels.
