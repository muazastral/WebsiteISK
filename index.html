About ISK

A small school with an international field of view.

International School of Kuantan serves families on Malaysia's East Coast with an English-medium learning environment, international accreditation, and a close community where students are known by name.

The 2026 prospectus positions ISK around Cambridge Curriculum progression, an IPC-enriched Primary programme, IGCSE preparation, structured enrichment, and academic pathways that continue beyond secondary school.

ISK's mission is rooted in holistic education, academic excellence, 21st Century competencies, and globally aware citizenship. Its vision is educational excellence tailored to the individual in a community of lifelong learners.

Admissions

Simple steps, clear fee snapshot, and human guidance.

Admissions can begin with a tour, enquiry, completed application form, registration fee, and documentation review. Families are typically updated after the application is processed.

  1. Talk to AdmissionsShare your child's age, schooling background, English level, and intended entry date.
  2. Visit or TrialTour the campus, meet the team, and experience the learning environment firsthand.
  3. Submit ApplicationComplete the admission form and provide the required student and guardian documents.
  4. Confirm PlacementPlacement is reviewed against age, readiness, previous schooling, and English proficiency.
