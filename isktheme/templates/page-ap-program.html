University-Level Courses

Advanced Placement® (AP) Program

AP offers college-level courses and exams while students are still in school. Strong AP results can support university applications and may lead to credit or placement, depending on each university's policy.

ISK presents 9 AP courses across languages, mathematics, sciences, and humanities for students ready for advanced academic challenge.

AP and College Board 9 AP subjects Exam scale 1 to 5

AP makes university-level challenge visible.

Students can build depth in chosen subjects, practise rigorous exam habits, and show universities that they are ready for advanced academic work.

Advantages

Why take AP courses at ISK?

University Credit

Strong AP results may help students earn credit or placement where universities recognise AP scores.

Academic Rigour

Develop strong critical thinking, problem-solving, and analytical skills essential for university success.

University Readiness

Gain exposure to university-level curriculum, easing the transition from high school to higher education.

Enhanced Applications

Demonstrate commitment to academic excellence and gain a competitive edge in university admissions.

Broadened Knowledge

Explore interests in-depth and expand knowledge beyond the standard curriculum.

Personal Growth

Foster a strong work ethic, time management skills, discipline, and a growth mindset.
