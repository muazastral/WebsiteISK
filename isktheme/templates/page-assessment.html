Assessment Framework

Clear, fair, and rigorous assessment that prepares students for global examinations.

How We Assess

A balanced framework for continuous growth.

ISK uses a three-part assessment model designed to prepare students for Cambridge external examinations while nurturing continuous improvement.

60%

End-of-Year Examinations

Formal exam papers aligned with Cambridge standards. Covers syllabus content from the entire academic year. Prepares students for the rigour of Checkpoint, IGCSE, and A-Level examinations.

30%

Coursework, Projects & Mock Exams

Extended assignments, laboratory reports, research projects, and mock examinations simulate real assessment conditions. Students receive detailed feedback to identify and improve on weaknesses.

10%

Regular In-Class Assessments

Weekly quizzes, class participation, presentations, and homework. Provides ongoing data for teachers to adjust instruction and for students to track their progress.

Cambridge Examinations

Internationally recognised qualifications at every stage.

Year 6 & Year 9

Cambridge Checkpoint

Diagnostic assessments in English, Mathematics, and Science. Provides international benchmarking against students worldwide. Results include detailed diagnostic feedback to guide future learning.

Cambridge IGCSE

Mock Examinations

Practice under real conditions.

Mock examinations are conducted under formal conditions full exam papers, strict time limits, and external-standard marking. Results form part of the 30% coursework component and are used for predicted grades when applying to universities.

1

External Examinations

Support & Guidance

Every student is supported.

Students who need additional support receive targeted intervention through small-group revision sessions, one-to-one teacher consultations, and personalised study plans. Our English Development Programme supports non-native speakers in building academic English proficiency.
