Year 7 to Year 9

Lower Secondary builds independent learners and responsible young leaders.

Students move from guided learning into stronger personal organisation, subject confidence, and meaningful participation in school life.

Middle Years Growth

Academic range with arts, athletics, and community.

Lower Secondary students are encouraged to become independent, responsible, and lifelong learners. The programme balances academic learning with arts and athletics so students experience a rounded school life.

Core subjects include English, foreign languages, Humanities, Mathematics, Science, Music, and Physical Education.

Subject Confidence

Students strengthen skills across language, humanities, mathematics, science, music, and physical education.

Independent Habits

Organisation, revision routines, accountability, inquiry, collaboration, and self-management become explicit priorities.

Leadership Readiness

Students practise communication, teamwork, decision-making, service, public speaking, and responsibility.

Creative Expression

Music, performance, school events, yearbook, and student-led activities help students develop voice and confidence.

Sports and Wellbeing

Physical education and house culture support fitness, discipline, resilience, and school spirit.

IGCSE Preparation

Lower Secondary builds the academic habits and subject foundations needed for Upper Secondary success.
