What's Included

Click a module to see what students learn.

Leadership & Statesmanship

Ranger Camp, Youth Leaders, Emerging Leaders, Leadership Camp

Students engage in project-based leadership experiences and practical skill-building. The programme strengthens the ability to plan, organise, and lead activities, building confidence in communication and public speaking. It also promotes teamwork, collaboration, responsibility, decision-making, and problem-solving skills.

Elite Sport & Discipline

Swimming, Equestrian, Golf, Tennis, Pickleball, Padel

ISK students embody performance discipline. Physical excellence, mental resilience, and emotional regulation are treated as foundational competencies. Structured sport and wellbeing programmes cultivate endurance, accountability and composure under pressure.

Innovation & Value Creation

Robotics & Coding, Dance / Sing / Theatre, Financial Literacy, Business Planning

This programme transforms students from passive learners into active creators and problem solvers through engaging, hands-on experiences that integrate technical knowledge with essential life skills. Students think creatively, apply learning to real-world challenges, and develop an entrepreneurial mindset.

Culture & Global Confidence

Japanese / Korean Language, multilingual education, international exposure

Pillar 4 aims to develop well-rounded students who possess strong cultural awareness, refined social skills, and confidence to engage effectively in both local and global environments. Through multilingual education, international exposure, leadership opportunities, and participation in academic and creative competitions.
