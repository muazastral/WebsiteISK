Year 10 to Year 11

High School prepares students for Cambridge success and life after ISK.

Upper Secondary brings subject mastery, digital learning, coursework, mock examinations, and pathway planning into one purposeful stage.

Upper Secondary

Rigour, confidence, and clear next steps.

ISK's Upper Secondary curriculum uses high-quality learning resources and digital tools to deepen understanding across subjects. Students build the confidence needed for higher education while developing curiosity, creativity, and independent learning.

Mock examinations and structured support prepare students for Cambridge external assessments, including Checkpoint and IGCSE examinations.

Languages

English First Language, English as a Second Language, Chinese as a Foreign Language, Malay as a Foreign Language, and elective languages subject to demand.

Mathematics

Mathematics and Additional Mathematics build analytical confidence and preparation for future academic pathways.

Sciences

Biology, Physics, Chemistry, and Combined Science options develop practical understanding and scientific reasoning.

Humanities

Geography and Global Perspectives help students understand place, society, evidence, and global questions.

Creative and Professional

Music and Physical Education support creative expression, performance, fitness, discipline, and confidence.

Future Planning

A Level, BTEC, Foundation, ACCA, ICAEW, and English support pathways help students see options beyond Year 11.

Assessment

Balanced assessment with preparation for external standards.

ISK's assessment framework combines examinations, coursework, projects, mock examinations, and regular checkpoints.

60%End-of-year examinations measure mastery and prepare students for Cambridge external assessments.
30%Coursework, project work, and mock examinations encourage research, creativity, and practical application.
10%In-class assessments and checkpoints provide timely feedback and continuous improvement.
