Join ISK

Simple steps, clear guidance, and a personal campus visit.

Families can begin with an enquiry, campus tour, trial experience, application form, document review, and placement confirmation.

  1. Talk to AdmissionsShare your child's age, schooling background, English level, and intended entry date.
  2. Visit or TrialTour the campus, meet the team, and experience the learning environment firsthand.
  3. Submit ApplicationComplete the admission form and provide student and guardian documents for review.
  4. Confirm PlacementPlacement considers age, readiness, previous schooling, and English proficiency.
