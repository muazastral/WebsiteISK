About ISK

A close international school community on Malaysia's East Coast.

International School of Kuantan serves families with an English-medium environment, international accreditation, and a learning culture that balances academic excellence with character, creativity, and global awareness.

The 2026 prospectus positions ISK around Cambridge Curriculum progression, IPC-inspired Primary learning, IGCSE preparation, English language development, enrichment pillars, summer school, school houses, and future academic pathways.

Why Kuantan

A welcoming coastal city for international learning.

Kuantan combines beaches, nature, cultural traditions, city convenience, and community warmth, giving students a calmer environment to live, learn, and grow.

  • Beaches and water sports
  • Nature and outdoor learning
  • Cultural festivals and heritage
  • Local food and markets
  • Shopping and city convenience
  • Warm community networks

Our Journey

Milestones that shaped ISK.

From founding to accreditation every step has built the school families trust today.

1996

Founded in Kuantan

International School of Kuantan opens its doors to serve families on Malaysia's East Coast.

2005

Cambridge Curriculum Adoption

ISK adopts the Cambridge pathway, providing internationally recognised academic structure.

2018

WASC Accreditation

Western Association of Schools and Colleges accreditation confirms ISK's quality standards.

2026

Future-Ready Campus

New prospectus, expanded pathways, and a campus designed for the next generation of learners.
