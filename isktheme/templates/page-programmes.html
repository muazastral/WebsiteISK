Learning Stages

A connected journey from playful foundations to Cambridge readiness.

ISK's academic structure supports children as they grow in confidence, language, curiosity, independence, leadership, and future pathway planning.

Programme Pages

Choose the stage that fits your child.

Placement considers age, previous schooling, developmental readiness, and English proficiency. The school may exercise flexibility where appropriate.

Age 5 to 6Kindergarten

Cute, warm, expressive learning for first routines, friendships, stories, phonics, numbers, art, and confidence.

 Year 1 to Year 6Primary School

IPC-inspired inquiry, English, Mathematics, Science, Bahasa Melayu, Mandarin, Digital Literacy, music, PE, and wellbeing.

 Year 7 to Year 9Lower Secondary

Independent learning, foreign languages, Humanities, Mathematics, Science, arts, athletics, and responsibility.

 Year 10 to Year 11High School

Cambridge IGCSE preparation, coursework, mock exams, digital learning, and university pathway planning.
Cambridge Curriculum Progression

ISK's 2026 prospectus frames the academic journey around Cambridge Curriculum progression from Primary through Secondary school, preparing students with subject knowledge, critical thinking, problem-solving, and independent learning.

English Language Development

Targeted support helps students build speaking, listening, reading, and writing confidence while continuing mainstream academic learning.

Holistic Development

Student-led clubs, leadership opportunities, sports, creative expression, and service experiences help students grow beyond academic results.

Assessment

Learning is measured through examinations, coursework, projects, mock examinations, checkpoints, and formative classroom feedback.

Pathways

Students can plan ahead through A Level, BTEC International Level 3, Foundation with Kaplan, ACCA, ICAEW, and other progression routes.
