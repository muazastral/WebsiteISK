Year 1 to Year 6

Primary School makes curiosity a daily learning habit.

A thematic, inquiry-led Primary experience inspired by international practice and supported by a strong English language environment.

International Primary Curriculum

Children connect subjects to the world around them.

The IPC helps children aged 5 to 11 become informed, globally competent, and future-ready learners. Through thematic units, teachers create engaging learning opportunities that connect subjects, personal growth, and international understanding.

Learning is structured but flexible, giving teachers room to adapt to childrens interests and level of understanding while still building clear knowledge, skills, and reflection.

IPC Learning Goals

Subject learning, personal learning, and international learning are woven together so students understand both content and context.

Parent Connection

IPC learning helps parents understand what children are learning in class and why it matters at home.

Assessment

Teachers and students use assessment to understand skill growth, knowledge development, and next steps.

Learning Culture

Primary classrooms encourage curiosity, creativity, collaboration, confidence, and a love of learning.
