Kindergarten Programme

Warm routines, joyful language, and readiness for Year 1.

The 2026 prospectus frames ISK as an accredited Cambridge Curriculum school from Year 1 to Year 11. Kindergarten gives young learners a caring start before that pathway, helping them grow comfortable with classroom routines, English communication, friendships, and guided exploration.

Placement is considered with care. The prospectus notes that student placement is based on age as of 31 August 2026, with flexibility for previous schooling, developmental readiness, and English proficiency.

  • Next pathway: Year 1 is listed for children age 5 turning 6.
  • Learning culture: curiosity, creativity, confidence, and a love of learning.
  • Family support: admissions can advise the best entry point for each child.
Real classroom community

Daily Learning

Small habits that help young children feel brave at school.

Kindergarten keeps the day warm, active, and language-rich so children can settle, try, speak, share, and belong.

Language

Stories, songs, speaking practice, listening habits, vocabulary, phonics exposure, and confident classroom communication.

Early Numeracy

Number sense, patterns, sorting, counting, comparisons, puzzles, and practical problem-solving through play.

Social Growth

Friendship, turn-taking, kindness, simple responsibility, emotional language, and safe classroom routines.

Creative Expression

Art, music, movement, role play, storytelling, performance, and confidence in front of a friendly audience.

Wellbeing

Children learn to care for themselves, ask for help, follow routines, and feel comfortable within the school community.

Global Confidence

ISK's multicultural environment helps young learners practise respect, curiosity, and joyful cross-cultural friendship.

A Kuantan Childhood

Learning feels close, caring, and full of wonder.

The prospectus describes Kuantan as a welcoming place to live, learn, and grow. Kindergarten brings that feeling into the classroom: calm routines, playful discovery, and a school community that helps children take their next step with confidence.
