Management Profile

Key people shaping ISK's direction and day-to-day quality.

The profiles below are concise web versions of the longer prospectus biographies. They can be expanded with portraits, formal greetings, and downloadable full profiles later.

Chief Executive Officer

Datin Ainul Latifah Ismail

A result-driven, multidisciplinary leader with more than 37 years of experience in corporate banking, finance, project management, administration, client accountability, and organisational expansion.

Her background includes major project coordination, financial analysis, internal control improvement, construction progress management, stakeholder communication, and quality-focused delivery across education-related developments.

Chief Operating Officer

Dato' Sazali Mohamad Salleh

A senior corporate and project development leader with more than 30 years of experience in architecture, construction, project delivery, business development, and operations.

His career includes founding and leading design, project management, and construction companies, with deep experience in school buildings and institutional facilities.

Principal

Dr. Kee Chin Hui

An education leader with more than a decade of experience in international schooling, teaching, curriculum development, school leadership, and accreditation.

Appointed Principal in 2023, Dr. Kee oversees academic and operational functions across K-12, including curriculum, teaching and learning, staff development, quality assurance, and WASC-related improvement practices.
