Primary Curriculum

International Primary Curriculum (IPC)

A thematic curriculum framework for primary-aged students, connecting subject learning with personal development and international mindedness.

ISK Primary Ages 5 to 11 Thematic Units

IPC turns a topic into a learning expedition.

Students begin with curiosity, gather what they already know, learn across subjects, reflect on personal goals, and finish by sharing their understanding with others.

IPC Learning Flow

1Entry PointLaunch the unit with experience.
2Knowledge HarvestFind what students already know.
3Subject LearningConnect science, humanities, arts, and language.
4International GoalsCompare perspectives and cultures.
5Exit PointShare learning with purpose.

About IPC

A global approach to primary learning.

IPC is organised through engaging thematic units, with learning goals for subject knowledge, personal development, and international understanding.

Global Perspective

The IPC was developed to provide an international perspective, organised around engaging thematic units that foster curiosity and connections between knowledge areas.

Holistic Development

Focuses on knowledge, skills, and understanding across subject areas, incorporating thematic units, cross-curricular learning, and personal development goals.

ISK Integration

At ISK, the IPC has been integrated to enhance learning and promote a global outlook. Students explore subjects through engaging thematic units like “Our Planet Earth.”

Learning Process

Thematic Learning Framework

Every IPC unit follows a structured learning journey that takes students from curiosity to mastery.

1
Entry Point

Engaging field trips, guest speakers, or hands-on activities that spark curiosity and set the context for learning.

2
Knowledge Harvest

Students identify existing knowledge through discussions, brainstorming, and research to establish a baseline.

3
Subject Focus

Subject-specific learning across Science, Geography, Social Studies, Language Arts, Mathematics, and Art.

4
Personal Development

Students focus on personal development goals related to the theme, building character alongside knowledge.

5
Exit Point

A student-led exhibition showcasing learning, solutions, and recommendations to the school community.

Key Features

What makes IPC unique?

Cross-Curricular

Subjects are taught through integrated thematic units, helping students see connections between different knowledge areas and apply learning in context.

Creative Expression

Art, music, and drama are woven into learning themes, encouraging students to express understanding through multiple forms of communication.

International Mindedness

Students develop respect for and understanding of different cultures, perspectives, and ways of life through carefully designed learning experiences.

Assessment for Learning

Continuous and formative assessment helps teachers understand each child's progress and tailor instruction to meet individual needs.

Home-School Connection

Parents are regularly informed about thematic units and invited to participate in entry and exit points, creating a strong learning partnership.

Personal Goals

Each unit includes personal development goals such as adaptability, communication, thoughtfulness, and resilience that build character.
