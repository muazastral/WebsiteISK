Official Test Centre

SAT® Testing Centre at ISK

ISK is an official SAT test centre (Centre No. 68180), offering the College Board SAT to prepare students for global university admissions.

Test Centre Details

Centre No. 68180

Test Centre: 68180

School: International School of Kuantan

Location: Kuantan, Pahang, Malaysia

Coordinator: nurita@isk.edu.my

Testing Time: Begins at 8:00 AM

Arrival: 7:00 – 7:30 AM

Required: Admission Ticket + MyKad/NRIC (Malaysian) or valid Passport (International) + Bluebook™-compatible laptop or tablet.

Centre No. 68180 Digital SAT Score range 400 to 1600

Two digital sections, one university-readiness signal.

The digital SAT is taken in Bluebook and uses Reading and Writing plus Math sections. Students should confirm test dates, device rules, and admission ticket details directly with College Board.

SAT Digital Blueprint

Reading and Writing64 minutes / 54 questions

Short passages and questions on information, craft, expression, and conventions.

Math70 minutes / 44 questions

Algebra, advanced math, problem solving, data analysis, geometry, and trigonometry.

About the SAT

A global standard for university admissions.

The SAT is widely used for college admissions in the United States and accepted by universities worldwide. It measures readiness for university-level coursework.

Global Acceptance

Accepted by thousands of universities worldwide, from the United States to the UK, Australia, and beyond, opening pathways to global higher education.

Two Core Sections

Reading and Writing plus Math. Total score range of 400 to 1600, with each section scored 200 to 800.

Digital Format

The SAT is now administered digitally via the Bluebook™ application, providing a streamlined testing experience with adaptive questioning.

University Scholarships

Strong SAT scores can unlock merit-based scholarships at universities worldwide, reducing the financial burden of higher education.

Multiple Test Dates

The College Board offers multiple SAT test dates throughout the academic year. ISK students register through the College Board website.

Score Reporting

Students can send scores to up to four universities for free on test day, with detailed score reports available online within weeks.

How to Register

Steps to take the SAT at ISK.

1
Create College Board Account

Visit collegeboard.org and create a student account to begin the registration process.

2
Select Test Date & Centre

Choose your preferred test date and select ISK (Centre No. 68180) as your test location.

3
Complete Registration

Fill in personal details, upload a photo, and pay the registration fee through the College Board portal.

4
Download Bluebook™

Install the Bluebook application on your laptop or tablet and complete the pre-test setup before test day.

5
Test Day

Arrive at ISK by 7:00 to 7:30 AM with your Admission Ticket, valid ID, and Bluebook-ready device.
