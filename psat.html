About PSAT

A stepping stone to college readiness.

Administered by the College Board, the PSAT/NMSQT gives students a practice opportunity for the SAT and, for eligible students, can connect to scholarship programmes.

Global Recognition

The PSAT gives students a clear preview of SAT-style reading, writing, and mathematics expectations.

Performance Insights

Detailed score reports identify strengths and areas for improvement across Reading and Writing and Math.

College Preparation

Prepares students for the SAT and the college application process by familiarising them with standardised test formats and question types.

Scholarship Opportunities

High scores on the PSAT/NMSQT may qualify students for National Merit Scholarship programs, providing financial aid for university study.

At ISK

How ISK prepares students for the PSAT.

Integrated Preparation

PSAT skills are embedded into the regular curriculum, with focused practice sessions in mathematics, reading, and language arts.

Practice Tests

Students take full-length practice tests under timed conditions to build familiarity, confidence, and effective test-taking strategies.

Progress Tracking

Teachers work with students to analyse PSAT practice results, set improvement goals, and provide targeted support for weaker areas.

Test Structure

What to expect on the PSAT.

Evidence-Based Reading & Writing

Digital Reading and Writing uses short passages and questions focused on information, craft, expression, and standard English conventions.

Section score range: 160 to 760

Mathematics

Math covers algebra, advanced math, problem solving, data analysis, geometry, and trigonometry in a digital format.

Section score range: 160 to 760
