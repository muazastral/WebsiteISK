Academic Pathways

Clear routes to university, applied study, and professional qualifications.

After the Cambridge secondary pathway, students can plan future study through academic, applied, foundation, and professional routes.

Cambridge A LevelA globally recognised university preparation route accepted by many institutions worldwide.
BTEC International Level 3A coursework-based, skills-oriented route for applied learning, career preparation, and degree progression.
Foundation with KaplanPreparatory programmes that strengthen academic skills and English before bachelor degree entry.
ACCA and ICAEWProfessional accounting and finance options for students looking toward global qualifications.
English Language DevelopmentTargeted support helps students grow speaking, listening, reading, and writing skills alongside mainstream learning.
Summer SchoolProject-based learning across STEM, creative arts, leadership, future careers, adventure, and language discovery.

Elective Programme

Four pillars for holistic student development.

The elective programme provides structured opportunities for leadership, discipline, creativity, global awareness, talent discovery, and practical learning.

Pillar 01

Leadership and Statesmanship

Student leadership, service, public speaking, teamwork, responsibility, and decision-making.

Pillar 02

Elite Sport and Discipline

Physical excellence, mental resilience, emotional regulation, accountability, and composure.

Pillar 03

Innovation and Value Creation

Hands-on problem solving, creative thinking, entrepreneurship, and practical solutions.

Pillar 04

Culture and Global Confidence

Multilingual education, international exposure, refinement, cultural sensitivity, and confidence.
